## 1. Maqsad
TZ §24 Parent Portal (farzandlar, davomat, natija, qarz, bildirishnoma) real markazda ishlashi uchun ota-onani tizimga kiritib,
farzandiga bog'lash kerak. Hozir bunday yo'l yo'q. Shu qadamda yana bir teshik yopiladi: `/users` orqali "yetim" student/ota-ona hisobi yaratish.

## 2. Hozirgi holat (kodda tekshirilgan)
- Entity'lar bor: `Modules.Students/Domain/Parent.cs` — `Parent(OrganizationId, UserId)` va `StudentParent(StudentId, ParentId)`.
  `parents.UserId` — **unique index** (`StudentsConfigurations`): bitta user bitta `Parent` qatori.
- `new Parent(` **faqat** `DbSeeder` va `DemoDataSeeder` da. Hech qanday endpoint ota-ona yaratmaydi yoki farzandga biriktirmaydi.
- Ota-ona qatori quyidagilarni oziqlantiradi: `StudentAccess` (scope `MyChildren`), `NotificationRecipients.ParentsAsync`,
  `DashboardController.Parent` (`/api/dashboard/parent`), moliya/davomat ko'rish. Qator bo'lmasa ota-ona kirsa **bo'sh** sahifa ko'radi.
- **Teshik:** `UsersController.Create` (`POST /api/users`) `roleCode = PARENT` yoki `STUDENT` bilan faqat `User` yaratadi — `Parent`/`Student` qatori yo'q.
  `BranchScopedAssignableRoles` ichida `Student` va `Parent` bor, ya'ni filial administratori ham yarata oladi; UI:
  `frontend/src/components/users/UserDirectory.tsx` yaratish oynasidagi rol tanlagichi (`/accounts` sahifasi). `POST /api/users/{id}/roles` (`AssignRole`) ham shunday.
  S01.01 faqat `/students` yo'lini qo'shdi, bu teshik ochiq qoldi.

## 3. Nima qilinadi

### 3.1. Domen va migratsiya
- `StudentParent` ga `Relationship` — yangi enum `ParentRelationship { Father, Mother, Guardian, Other }` (`Modules.Students/Domain/Parent.cs`), matn sifatida (`HasConversion<string>().HasMaxLength(20)`), standart `Guardian`.
  `StudentParent` konstruktoriga parametr qo'shing.
- Migratsiya: **`AddParentRelationship`**.
- Yangi timeline turlari: `PARENT_LINKED`, `PARENT_UNLINKED` (`TimelineEventType`) + frontend `EVENT_CONFIG`.
- Yangi bildirishnoma turi: `NotificationType.ParentLinked = "PARENT_LINKED"`.

### 3.2. Backend — `Api/Students/StudentParentsController.cs` (`[Route("api/students")]`)
| Endpoint | Ruxsat | Nima qiladi |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/students/{id}/parents` | `students.view` | vasiylar: ism, telefon, email, munosabat, `userId`. `CanAccessStudentAsync` |
| `POST /api/students/{id}/parents` | `parents.manage` *(yangi)* | **ikki rejim**: (a) mavjud `userId` (u `PARENT` rolida bo'lishi shart) yoki (b) yangi hisob: `fullName`, `phone`, `email`, `password`, `relationship` |
| `DELETE /api/students/{id}/parents/{parentId}` | `parents.manage` | bog'lamni uzadi; `Parent` qatori **qoladi** (boshqa farzandi bo'lishi mumkin) |

Qoidalar:
- Yangi hisob: `PasswordPolicy.Validate`, email takrorlanmasin (409), email `MailAddress` bilan tekshirilsin (S01.01 dagi `IsValidEmail` ni umumiy yordamchiga chiqaring), rol `PARENT`, `Parent` qatori yaratiladi,
  **filial**: studentning faol enrollmentlari guruhlari filiallari (`user.AssignBranch(...)` har biriga).
- Mavjud `userId`: rol `PARENT` emas → 400; `Parent` qatori yo'q bo'lsa yaratiladi; shu student bilan bog'lam allaqachon bor → 409.
- Scope: `scope.CanAccessStudentAsync(db, id)` (403), tashkilot tekshiruvi (404). `Operator` va `Administrator` faqat o'z filiali studentiga.
- Timeline `PARENT_LINKED` (payload: ota-ona ismi, munosabat, `byUserId`); ota-onaga bildirishnoma: "{student} farzandingiz sifatida biriktirildi" + havola `/dashboard`.
- Yangi ota-ona hisobi uchun javobda parol **qaytarilmaydi** (frontend o'zi kiritgan parolni bir marta ko'rsatadi — `StudentCreateDrawer` kabi).

### 3.3. Teshikni yopish — `UsersController`
- `Create`: `roleCode == STUDENT` → **400** ("Student `/students` sahifasidan yaratiladi — u yerda guruh va profil ham yaratiladi."). `roleCode == PARENT` → `User` bilan birga `Parent` qatorini ham yarating.
- `AssignRole` (`POST /api/users/{id}/roles`): xuddi shu — `STUDENT` rad etiladi; `PARENT` berilganda `Parent` qatori bo'lmasa qo'shiladi.
- `UserDirectory.tsx` rol tanlagichidan `STUDENT` ni olib tashlang (yoki tanlanganda "Students sahifasidan yarating" havolasi ko'rsating).
- Mavjud testlar (masalan `ScopeAuthorizationTests`) STUDENT rolli user yaratayotgan bo'lishi mumkin — ularni `/api/students` ga o'tkazing; `grep -rn '"STUDENT"' backend/tests`.

### 3.4. Ruxsat
`parents.manage` — SA `AllOrganization`, AD `MyBranches`, OP `MyBranches` (`PermissionSeedData` + `docs/01` matritsasi).

### 3.5. Frontend
- `lib/api/students.ts`: `listStudentParents`, `addStudentParent` (ikki rejim uchun ikki funksiya), `removeStudentParent`; tur `StudentParentLink`.
- `components/StudentParents.tsx`: student profilida **"Umumiy"** tab ichida karta (alohida tab emas — ko'pi bilan 2–3 qator): ism, munosabat, telefon, ✕ (uzish, `ConfirmDialog` bilan);
  "Ota-ona qo'shish" → `ParentLinkDrawer` (tab: "Yangi hisob" / "Mavjud hisob"; mavjud hisob uchun `GET /api/users?role=PARENT&search=`). Hammasi `can("parents.manage")` bilan.
- Yangi hisob yaratilgach login/parol bir marta ko'rsatiladi (namuna: `StudentCreateDrawer.tsx` muvaffaqiyat paneli).
- `students/[id]/page.tsx`: sarlavhaga birinchi ota-onaning telefonini ("Ota-ona: Ism · +998…") qo'shing (kontakt yo'q bo'lsa ko'rsatmang).

## 4. Testlar — `StudentParentsTests.cs` (~10)
1. Yangi ota-ona yaratish + bog'lash → ota-ona login qiladi, `GET /api/dashboard/parent` da farzand ko'rinadi.
2. Mavjud `PARENT` foydalanuvchini biriktirish; rolsiz (masalan teacher) userId → 400; takroriy bog'lam → 409.
3. Bitta ota-ona ikki farzandga — dashboard ikkalasini ko'rsatadi.
4. Uzish: bog'lam yo'qoladi, `Parent` qatori qoladi, ota-ona endi farzandni ko'rmaydi (`GET /api/students/{id}` → 403).
5. Teacher → 403; administrator boshqa filial studentiga → 403 (POST ham, GET ham).
6. `POST /api/users` `STUDENT` → 400; `PARENT` → 201 va u login qilib `/api/dashboard/parent` dan **bo'sh ro'yxat** oladi (xato emas).
7. `POST /api/users/{id}/roles` `STUDENT` → 400.
8. Timeline'da `PARENT_LINKED`; ota-onada bildirishnoma.
