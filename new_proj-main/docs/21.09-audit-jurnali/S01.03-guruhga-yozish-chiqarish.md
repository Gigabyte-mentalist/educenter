## 1. Maqsad
Student hayot siklining oxirgi qadamlari kodda yo'q: mavjud studentni ikkinchi guruhga yozib bo'lmaydi, guruhdan chiqarib bo'lmaydi,
`EnrollmentStatus.Dropped`, `StudentStatus.Graduated`, `StudentStatus.Dropped` **hech qachon yozilmaydi** (TZ §10 ning oxirgi holatlari erishib bo'lmaydigan).

## 2. Hozirgi holat (kodda tekshirilgan)
- `new Enrollment(` faqat: lid konvertatsiyasi (`LeadsController.Convert`), `EnrollmentLifecycleController.Transfer`, `StudentAdminController.Create`, seederlar.
- `EnrollmentStatus`: `Active`, `Frozen`, `Transferred`, `Dropped` (`Modules.Students/Domain/Enrollment.cs`) — **`Completed` yo'q**. `Enrollment.SetStatus` bor.
- `StudentStatus`: `Active`, `Debt`, `Frozen`, `Transferred`, `Graduated`, `Dropped`. `BillingRules` (`Api/Automation/BillingRules.cs`, ~180-qator) `Active ↔ Debt` ni **faqat** invoice holatiga qarab almashtiradi —
  bu status Active ustidagi "qoplama". Yangi mantiq `Debt` ni bexosdan `Active` ga aylantirib yubormasin.
- `BillingRules` faqat `Active` enrollment'ga invoice yozadi → chiqarish/bitirish hisob-kitobni **o'zi** to'xtatadi. Ochiq invoice'lar tegilmaydi (o'qilgan davr uchun) — transfer/freeze bilan bir xil qaror.
- **Topilgan kamchilik:** `LeadsController.Convert` (va `BookTrial`) faqat `EnsureGroupInScopeAsync` ni ishlatadi — guruh **to'lganmi/yopilganmi** tekshirilmaydi.
  S01.01 dagi `EnrollmentRules.CheckAsync` shuni to'g'ri qiladi.
- Guruh chati: `ChatAccess` faqat `EnrollmentStatus.Active` ga ruxsat beradi → enrollment yopilganda chatga kirish o'zi yo'qoladi (alohida "arxivlash" kerak emas).

## 3. Nima qilinadi

### 3.1. Domen va migratsiya
- `EnrollmentStatus.Completed` qo'shing (migratsiya kerak emas — matn).
- `Enrollment` ga `EndedAt` (`DateTimeOffset?`) va `EndReason` (`string?`, 500) + `End(EnrollmentStatus status, string? reason)` metodi (`Dropped`/`Completed`/`Transferred` uchun `EndedAt = UtcNow`).
  `Transfer`/`Freeze` ham `EndedAt` qo'ysin? — **faqat `Transferred`** (freeze vaqtinchalik). Migratsiya: **`AddEnrollmentEnd`**.
- Yangi timeline turlari: `ENROLLMENT_DROPPED`, `GRADUATED`; bildirishnoma: `NotificationType.EnrollmentDropped`, `Graduated`.
- **`StudentStatusRules.Recompute(Student, IEnumerable<Enrollment>)`** (yangi, `Api/Students/`): faol enrollment bor → (`Debt` bo'lsa **`Debt` qoladi**, aks holda `Active`); faqat `Frozen` bor → `Frozen`;
  hech biri faol/muzlatilgan emas va oxirgisi `Completed` → `Graduated`; oxirgisi `Dropped` → `Dropped`. Transfer/Freeze/Resume ham shundan foydalansin (hozir ular statusni qo'lda qo'yadi) — **lekin ularning testlari o'zgarmasin**.

## 4. Testlar — `EnrollmentLifecycleTests.cs` ga +~10 (yoki yangi `EnrollmentManagementTests.cs`)
1. Ikkinchi guruhga yozish (student ikki faol enrollmentda). 2. Takroriy yozish → 409. 3. To'lgan guruh → 409. 4. Yopiq guruh → 409.
5. Drop: sabab yo'q → 400; bor → `Dropped`; **oxirgi** faol enrollment tushsa student `Dropped`, boshqasi qolsa `Active`.
6. Graduate → hamma enrollment `Completed`, student `Graduated`; keyin qayta `enrollments` → student `Active`.
7. Drop'dan keyin yangi invoice chiqmaydi (`BillingRules.RunAsync`ni chaqirib — `AutomationTests` dagi naqsh). 8. `Debt` student yangi guruhga yozilsa `Debt` qoladi.
9. Teacher → 403; administrator boshqa filial studenti → 403; operator → 200.
10. Lid konvertatsiyasi to'lgan guruhga → 409 (yangi xatti-harakat).
Mavjud 9 ta lifecycle testi o'zgarmasdan o'tishi shart.
