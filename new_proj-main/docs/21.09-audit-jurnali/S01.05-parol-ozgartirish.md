## 1. Maqsad
Hozir hech kim parolini o'zgartira olmaydi, administrator ham boshqaning parolini tiklay olmaydi (`AuthController`: faqat `login`/`refresh`/`logout`).
Student/ota-onaga vaqtinchalik parol berilgani uchun (S01.01, S01.02) birinchi kirishda **o'zgartirishga majburlash** ham kerak.

> Bu qadamda **OTP bilan "parolni unutdim"** yo'q — u Email/SMS kanalini talab qiladi (S06.03 → S06.04).

## 2. Hozirgi holat
- `AuthController` (`Login`, `Refresh`, `Logout`), `JwtTokenService`, `RefreshToken` (`Revoke()`, `IsActive`).
- `Api/Auth/PasswordPolicy.Validate` bor (S01.01). Parol xeshi `PasswordHasher<User>` (ASP.NET Identity), `User.SetPasswordHash`.
- `PATCH /api/users/{id}` faqat `FullName`/`Phone`. Email/parol o'zgartirish yo'q.
- Audit: `User` auditlanadi, `PasswordHash` **yashirin** (`RedactedProperties`). `PasswordChangedAt`/`MustChangePassword` esa audit jurnalida ko'rinadi (yaxshi).
- S01.04 dan keyin: `AccountStatusMiddleware` va `UserStatusCache` mavjud — majburiy o'zgartirishni shu yerda **serverda** ham majburlaymiz.

## 3. Nima qilinadi

### 3.1. Domen va migratsiya
- `User.PasswordChangedAt` (`DateTimeOffset?`), `User.MustChangePassword` (`bool`, standart `false`); metodlar: `ChangePassword(string hash)` (`PasswordChangedAt = UtcNow`, `MustChangePassword = false`),
  `RequirePasswordChange()`. Migratsiya: **`AddPasswordChangeTracking`**.
- Yangi ruxsat: **`users.password.reset`** — SA `AllOrganization`, AD `MyBranches` (Operator yo'q). `users.edit` ni ishlatmang: u Teacher/Student/Parent'ga `Self` scope bilan berilgan.

### 3.2. Backend
| Endpoint | Ruxsat | Nima qiladi |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /api/auth/change-password` | `[Authorize]` (rate limit: `LoginBruteForce`) | `{ currentPassword, newPassword }`. Joriy parol noto'g'ri → 400; `PasswordPolicy`; yangi parol joriyga teng → 400. Xesh almashadi, `MustChangePassword=false`. **Barcha** refresh tokenlar bekor qilinadi (`ExecuteUpdateAsync`, `RevokedAt`) va **yangi juftlik qaytariladi** (`TokenResponse`) — joriy sessiya davom etadi, boshqa qurilmalar chiqib ketadi |
| `POST /api/users/{id}/reset-password` | `users.password.reset` | `{ newPassword? }`. Bo'sh bo'lsa server 12 belgili parol yaratadi (`PasswordGenerator`: `frontend/src/lib/generate-password.ts` dagi alifbo, `RandomNumberGenerator`). `MustChangePassword=true`, tokenlar bekor qilinadi, kesh yangilanadi (`AccountBlocker.StatusChangedAsync`). Javob: `{ temporaryPassword }` — **faqat shu safar** |
- `reset-password`: o'zini tiklay olmaydi (400: "o'z parolingiz uchun «Parolni o'zgartirish»"); **rolga sezgir `CanTouchUser`** (filial administratori Super Admin/Administrator/Developer parolini tiklay olmaydi — 403).
- `TokenResponse` (`Api/Auth/AuthDtos.cs`) ga `MustChangePassword` (bool) qo'shing; `Login`/`Refresh` uni to'ldiradi.
- **Server tomondan majburlash:** `UserStatusCache` qiymatini `AccountState(UserStatus Status, bool MustChangePassword)` ga kengaytiring; `AccountStatusMiddleware`: `MustChangePassword` va yo'l `/api/auth` bilan boshlanmasa →
  `403 { error: "Avval vaqtinchalik parolni o'zgartiring.", code: "PASSWORD_CHANGE_REQUIRED" }` (`ApiErrorCodes.PasswordChangeRequired`).
- Audit: alohida yozuv kerak emas (interceptor `User` o'zgarishini yozadi, xesh yashirin). Lekin **parol matni hech qayerga loglanmasin**.

### 3.3. Frontend
- `lib/api/auth.ts`: `changePassword(current, next)` → `TokenResponse`; `TokenResponse.mustChangePassword`.
- `app/(auth)/change-password/page.tsx` — majburiy o'zgartirish ekrani (token bor, lekin `(admin)` layout emas): joriy parol, yangi parol (+takror), "Saqlash" → yangi tokenlar `saveTokens` → `/dashboard`.
- `login/page.tsx`: javobda `mustChangePassword` bo'lsa `router.push("/change-password")`.
- `api-client.ts`: `403` + `PASSWORD_CHANGE_REQUIRED` → `/change-password` ga yo'naltirish (S01.04 dagi `account-status` do'koniga o'xshash kichik hodisa yoki `window.location.assign`).
- `app/(admin)/settings/security/page.tsx` — "Parolni o'zgartirish" formasi (`UserMenu` dan havola: `components/UserMenu.tsx`). (S09.05 sessiyalar jadvalini shu sahifaga qo'shadi.)
- `users/[id]/page.tsx`: "Parolni tiklash" tugmasi (`can("users.password.reset")`, `ConfirmDialog`) → natija modalida vaqtinchalik parol **bir marta**, "nusxa olish" tugmasi bilan.
- Student/ota-ona yaratganda (S01.01/S01.02) vaqtinchalik parol berilganini hisobga oling: ularning yaratish endpointlari `MustChangePassword = true` qo'ysin? **Ha** — `StudentAdminController.Create` va ota-ona yaratish (S01.02) `user.RequirePasswordChange()` chaqirsin; `UsersController.Create` ham. (Testlar login qilib davom etadigan bo'lsa — yangi parolni almashtirish yoki bayroqni testda o'chirish kerak; mavjud testlar buzilmasligi uchun `LeadsController.Convert` va seederlarga **tegmang**.)

## 4. Testlar — `AuthTests.cs` ga +~8 yoki yangi `PasswordTests.cs`
1. `change-password`: to'g'ri → 200, yangi tokenlar; eski parol bilan login → 401; yangi bilan → 200.
2. Joriy parol xato → 400; qisqa parol → 400; yangi = joriy → 400.
3. O'zgartirgandan keyin **eski refresh token** ishlamaydi (401), qaytgan yangi juftlik ishlaydi.
4. `reset-password` (superadmin → teacher): vaqtinchalik parol qaytadi; shu parol bilan login → `mustChangePassword: true`; tiklangan foydalanuvchining eski tokenlari bekor.
5. `MustChangePassword` bo'lgan foydalanuvchi boshqa endpointga (`GET /api/students`) → **403 `PASSWORD_CHANGE_REQUIRED`**, `change-password` ga → 200 va shundan keyin oddiy ishlaydi.
6. Administrator → `superadmin@` parolini tiklashga → 403; o'zini tiklash → 400; teacher/student → 403.
7. Audit jurnalida parol xeshi yo'qligi (mavjud regressiya testi — `AuditRegressionTests` ni tekshiring).
