## 1. Maqsad
TZ §5.2 va §7 **Grammar %** va **Vocabulary %** ni talab qiladi ("Grammar: 71%", "Vocabulary: 84%"). `ExamResult` da faqat IELTS bo'limlari bor. Bu ikki ustunsiz Learning Profile,
filial Academic dashboardi va Teacher KPI hisoblanmaydi — shuning uchun bu "eng pastdagi g'isht", qolgan S04–S05 dan oldin.

## 2. Hozirgi holat (kodda tekshirilgan)
- `Modules.Lms/Domain/Exam.cs`: `ExamResult(OrganizationId, ExamId, StudentId, Listening?, Reading?, Writing?, Speaking?, Overall?)` — hammasi `decimal?` (IELTS band 0–9).
- `Api/Lms/ExamsController.cs`: `ExamResultDto(Id, ExamId, StudentId, Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking, Overall, StudentName?)`, `RecordExamResultRequest(StudentId, Listening?, ..., Overall?)` — `POST {id}/results` (upsert, `exams.score.edit`).
- `Api/Students/StudentLearningController.cs`: `StudentExamRow(Id, ExamName, GroupName, Date, Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking, Overall)`.
- Frontend: `app/(admin)/exams/page.tsx` (natijalar drawer'i), `components/StudentLearningTabs.tsx` (`StudentExamsTab`), `lib/api/lms.ts`.
- Mavjud natijalar (demo seed ~500) Grammar/Vocabulary'siz qoladi — **bu to'g'ri**: eski imtihonlarda ular o'lchanmagan. UI `null` ni "—" deb ko'rsatsin.

## 3. Nima qilinadi
- **Domen:** `ExamResult.Grammar` va `Vocabulary` — `decimal?`, **foiz 0–100** (IELTS bandlaridan farqli: TZ misollari foizda). Konstruktor va yangilash metodiga qo'shing (mavjud chaqiruvlar: `ExamsController`, `DbSeeder`, `DemoDataSeeder` — parametrlarni ixtiyoriy qiling, seederlar buzilmasin).
  Migratsiya **`AddExamGrammarVocabulary`**.
- **Validatsiya:** foiz `0..100` (aks holda 400). Band ustunlari uchun mavjud validatsiyani (0–9?) tekshiring va o'zgartirmang.
- **Backend:** `ExamResultDto`, `RecordExamResultRequest`, `StudentExamRow` ga ikki maydon (oxiriga, ixtiyoriy — mavjud testlar/klientlar buzilmasin).
- **Frontend:** imtihon natijalari drawer'iga va student "Imtihonlar" tabiga ikki ustun ("Grammatika %", "Lug'at %"); kiritish formasida ikki maydon; `null` → "—". IELTS turidagi imtihonda bu ikki maydon ixtiyoriy (bo'sh qoladi).
- (Ixtiyoriy) demo seeder: `DemoDataSeeder` da `Unit`/`Monthly` imtihonlar uchun Grammar/Vocabulary generatsiya qiling — keyingi qadamlarda (Learning Profile, filial dashboardi) haqiqiy ko'rinish uchun. `Seed:BulkDemoData` bayrog'i ortida qoladi.
