## 1. Maqsad
TZ §7 da student profilida 12 ta tab bor; hozir 9 ta: Umumiy, Davomat, Homework, Imtihonlar, To'lovlar, Support, Hujjatlar, Muloqot, Tarix. **Yo'q: Learning, Writing, Speaking.**

## 2. Hozirgi holat (kodda tekshirilgan)
- Tablar `app/(admin)/students/[id]/page.tsx` da (`Tabs`), ba'zilari `usePermissions`/rol funksiyalari bilan shartlangan (`canSeeHomework`, `canViewSupport`, `canViewStudentDocuments`, `canSeeFinance`).
- **Writing:** xodim uchun `GET /api/ai/writing/reviews?studentId=` (mavjud, `ai.writing.review` — SA, AD, Teacher, SupportTeacher; scope bilan) — o'zgartirish **kerak emas**. Student o'zi uchun `GET /api/ai/writing/evaluations` (`ai.writing.use`). Batafsil: `GET /api/ai/writing/evaluations/{id}`.
  Frontend: `app/(admin)/ai/writing/reviews/page.tsx` (o'qituvchi ro'yxati) — komponentlarini qayta ishlating.
- **Speaking — ATAYLAB SHAXSIY:** `Api/Ai/Speaking/SpeakingController.cs` sinf izohi: *"Sessions, recordings and assessments belong to the account that made them; nothing here is shared with staff."*
  `GET /api/ai/speaking/sessions` faqat `s.UserId == scope.UserId`; `ai.speaking.use` faqat **Student** rolida.
- `GET /api/students/{id}/learning-profile` — S04.02.

## 3. ⚠️ Avval foydalanuvchidan so'rang — Speaking tabi
Speaking mashqlari o'quvchining shaxsiy mashq maydoni sifatida yaratilgan (yozuv, transkript, baho — hech biri xodimga ko'rinmaydi). TZ §7 esa profilda "Speaking" tabini talab qiladi. Yagona to'g'ri yo'l — **mahsulot qarorini so'rash**
(`AskUserQuestion`): variantlar va tavsiya:

| Variant | Xodim nimani ko'radi | Izoh |
|---|---|---|
| **A (tavsiya)** | faqat **yakunlangan sessiyalarning baholari**: sana, rejim, taxminiy band, fluency/lexical/grammar bandlari, davomiyligi. **Audio va transkript YO'Q** | TZ talabini qoplaydi, shaxsiy yozuvlar himoyalangan |
| B | hammasi (audio + transkript + baho) | maxfiylik qarorini bekor qiladi — student ogohlantirilishi kerak |
| C | Speaking tabi umuman qo'shilmaydi; Learning profildagi Speaking faqat imtihondan | hozirgi holat saqlanadi |
Javobga qarab: A → yangi `GET /api/students/{id}/speaking-summary` (`students.view`, `CanAccessStudentAsync`; audio/transkript **qaytmaydi**) + student ogohlantirish matni ("Baholaringiz o'qituvchiga ko'rinadi") mashq sahifasida (`ai/speaking/page.tsx`);
B → ehtiyotkorlik bilan, alohida ruxsat (`ai.speaking.review`) va audio endpointi; C → faqat Learning + Writing.
**Javob kelmaguncha Speaking tabini yozmang** — qolgan ikki tabni tugating va Speaking'ni qarorga qoldirib jurnalga yozing.

## 4. Nima qilinadi

### 4.1. Learning tabi
`StudentLearningProfile` (S04.02) komponenti; tab ruxsati: `students.view` (student o'zi uchun ham — profilini ko'radi). Tab tartibi: Umumiy, **Learning**, Davomat, Homework, Imtihonlar, **Writing**, [Speaking], To'lovlar…

### 4.2. Writing tabi
- Xodim: `listWritingReviews({ studentId })` (mavjud `lib/api/ai.ts`) — sana, mavzu, taxminiy band, o'qituvchi bahosi bo'lsa "Tuzatilgan" belgisi, holat (`Completed/Queued/Failed`); qatorni bosish → batafsil (mavjud `ai/writing/reviews` sahifasidagi detail komponenti).
  Ruxsat: `can("ai.writing.review")`.
- Student o'z profilida: `listMyEvaluations()`; ruxsat `can("ai.writing.use")`.
- Bo'sh holat: "Hali insho yozilmagan".
- Trend mini-grafik (oxirgi 8 ta band) — ixtiyoriy, `recharts` hali qo'shilmagan bo'lsa **qo'shmang** (S05.03 da kiritiladi) — oddiy raqamlar qatori yetarli.

### 4.3. Speaking tabi — faqat qaror A bo'lsa (3-bo'lim)
`speaking-summary` endpointi va jadval; audio/transkript yo'q.

## 5. Testlar
- Writing: yangi endpoint yo'q — mavjud `WritingEvaluationTests` qoplaydi; frontend'da `can(...)` shartlarini brauzerda tekshiring (teacher ko'radi; student o'zinikini; operator ko'rmaydi).
- Speaking (A): `SpeakingSummaryTests` — teacher o'z guruh studentining bandlarini ko'radi; **javobda audio/transkript maydonlari yo'q** (JSON kalitlarini tekshiring); boshqa guruh → 403; student → o'zinikini.
