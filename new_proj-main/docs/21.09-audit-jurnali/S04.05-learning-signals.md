## 1. Maqsad
TZ §7 "Learning Signals" 6 ta; ishlaydiganlari 2 ta (**Attendance → 2 ketma-ket yo'qlik**, **Homework → bajarilish pasaymoqda** — `FollowUpRules`). Qolgan 4 tasi uchun ma'lumot **bor**, faqat qoida yozilmagan:

| Signal | Ma'lumot manbai |
|---|---|
| Writing → Grammar weak | `WritingEvaluation.GrammarBand` (o'qituvchi tuzatgani `TeacherGrammarBand` ustun) |
| Speaking → Low fluency | `SpeakingSession.FluencyBand` — ⚠️ **shaxsiy** (quyiga qarang) |
| Listening → Multiple low results | `ExamResult.Listening` |
| Reading → Time management issue | `ExamResult.Reading` boshqa bo'limlardan sezilarli past |

## 2. Hozirgi holat (kodda tekshirilgan)
- `Api/Automation/AutomationCatalog.cs`: `AutomationCatalog.All` — qoidalar massivi. Qoida: `new(AutomationRuleCode.X, nom, trigger, tavsif, [parametrlar (Key, Label, Unit, Min, Max, Default)], [amallar (Key, Label, Default)])`;
  kalitlar `AutomationCatalog.Keys`; `Resolve` saqlangan qiymatni katalog oralig'iga qisadi. Mavjud qoidalar: `TrialFollowUp`, `AbsenceFollowUp`, `HomeworkSupportSignal`, `DebtFollowUp`, `PaymentReminders`, `HomeworkDeadlineReminder`, `LessonReminder`. Kodlar: `Modules.Automation/Domain/AutomationRule.cs` (`AutomationRuleCode`).
- `Api/Automation/FollowUpRules.cs`: `RunSweepAsync` → `SweepResult(TrialFollowUps, AbsenceSignals, HomeworkSignals)`; `RunHomeworkSignalsAsync` — namuna: idempotentlik **trigger vaqtiga** nisbatan ("shu vaqt yoki undan keyin case yaratilgan bo'lsa jim"), `SupportCase` yaratadi (`SupportCaseSource.System`, `SignalCode`, `Reason` ≤ 500) + administratorga bildirishnoma (`SupportRecommended`).
- `AutomationSweepJob` (Hangfire, har necha daqiqada) `RunSweepAsync` ni chaqiradi; `/automation` sahifasi katalogdan **avtomatik** quriladi — frontend o'zgarishi kerak emas. `SupportCase.Reason` `/support` da ko'rinadi.
- ⚠️ **Speaking shaxsiy**: `SpeakingController` izohi — "nothing here is shared with staff". Fluency signali shaxsiy sessiya ma'lumotidan xodimga ko'rinadigan `SupportCase` yaratadi — bu S04.03 dagi qarorga bog'liq.

## 3. Nima qilinadi
Har qoida: kod (`AutomationRuleCode`), katalog yozuvi, `FollowUpRules` ichida metod, `SweepResult` kengayishi, sozlanadigan parametrlar:

| Qoida kodi | Shart | Parametrlar (min–max, standart) |
|---|---|---|
| `WritingGrammarSignal` | oxirgi **N** ta yakunlangan Writing baholashida grammatika bandi **M** dan past | N 2–10 (3), M 3.0–7.0 (5.5) |
| `ListeningLowSignal` | oxirgi **N** ta imtihonda Listening **M** dan past | N 2–6 (2), M 3.0–7.0 (5.0) |
| `ReadingTimeSignal` | oxirgi imtihonda Reading boshqa bo'limlar o'rtachasidan **D** banddan past | D 0.5–3.0 (1.0) |
| `SpeakingFluencySignal` | **faqat S04.03 dagi qaror ruxsat bersa** — oxirgi N sessiyada fluency M dan past/pasaymoqda | N 2–6 (3), M 3.0–7.0 (5.0) |
- Amallar (hammasida): `RaiseSupport` ("Support tavsiyasi (administratorga xabar bilan)", standart yoqiq), `NotifyTeacher` ("Asosiy o'qituvchiga bildirishnoma", standart yoqiq).
- `SignalCode` qiymatlari (mavjudlarga o'xshash, ≤ 40): `WRITING_GRAMMAR_WEAK`, `LISTENING_LOW`, `READING_TIME`, `SPEAKING_FLUENCY_LOW`. `Reason` matni o'zbekcha va **aniq raqamli**: "Oxirgi 3 ta insho: grammatika 5.0, 5.0, 4.5 (chegara 5.5)".
- Idempotentlik: **mavjud naqshdagidek** trigger vaqtiga nisbatan (oxirgi baholash/imtihon sanasi). Bitta studentga bir xil signal uchun ochiq `SupportCase` bo'lsa yangisi yaratilmaydi.
- Ma'lumot yetmasa (N dan kam natija) — signal chiqmaydi.
- **Speaking signali**: qaror B/C bo'lsa **yozmang**; A bo'lsa ham `Reason` da transkript/audio mazmunini **emas**, faqat band raqamini keltiring.
- Hisoblash: qaysi skillga "past" deyish — `LearningProfileCalculator` (S04.02) dagi yordamchilardan foydalaning, alohida formula o'ylab topmang.
