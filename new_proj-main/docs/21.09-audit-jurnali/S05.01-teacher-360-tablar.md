## 1. Maqsad
TZ §6: Teacher 360° da 13 tab. Hozir 4 ta (**Guruhlar, Studentlar, Jadval, Natijadorlik**) + sarlavhada "Xabar yozish". Bu qadamda **Overview, Attendance, Homework, Exam Results, Support Teachers, Activity History** qo'shiladi.
(**Feedback** va **KPI** — Faza 6: S10.02, S10.01. **Portfolio** — S05.02.)

## 2. Hozirgi holat (kodda tekshirilgan)
- `Api/Users/TeachersController.cs`: `GET /api/teachers` (`TeacherListDto`, `CanOpen`), `GET {id}` (`TeacherOverviewDto`: guruhlar, studentlar), `GET {id}/performance` (`TeacherPerformanceDto`: `ActiveStudents, GroupsCount, AttendanceRate, HomeworkCompletionRate, AverageExamScore`). Hammasi `users.view`.
  Scope qoidasi (`GetById`): o'zi **yoki** keng scope (`AllOrganization`/`MyBranches` — va filial ustma-ust tushishi). Boshqalar (hamkasb) — o'z sahifasini ochadi, boshqasini emas.
- `frontend/src/app/(admin)/teachers/[id]/page.tsx` — `Tabs`: groups, students, schedule (`TeacherSchedule`), performance (`TeacherPerformanceTab`). Sarlavha: `PageHeader` + `OpenChatButton`.
- **O'qituvchining o'z davomati (punctuality) kuzatilmaydi** — infratuzilma yo'q (S10.01 da `Lesson.StartedAt`). Bu qadamdagi "Attendance" — **uning guruhlaridagi studentlar davomati**.
- Audit: `GET /api/audit-logs` (`audit.log.view`, SA/AD) — sahifalangan, filtrlar `GET /api/audit-logs/filters`; **`actorId` bo'yicha filtr bor-yo'qligini `AuditLogsController.List` da tekshiring**.

## 3. Nima qilinadi

### 3.1. Backend
| Endpoint | Ruxsat | Nima qaytaradi |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/teachers/{id}/workload?from=&to=` | `users.view` (+ mavjud teacher scope qoidasi) | bitta javobda: **guruhlar bo'yicha** davomat % (bekor qilingan darslarsiz), o'tkazilgan darslar soni; **homework**: yaratilgan, tekshirilmagan soni, tekshirish o'rtacha vaqti (soat: `ReviewedAt − SubmittedAt`); **imtihon**: guruh bo'yicha o'rtacha `Overall` va trend (oylik); |
| `GET /api/teachers/{id}/support-teachers` | `users.view` | uning guruhlaridagi `SupportTeacher` xodimlar (ism, qaysi guruhda) va aksincha (agar u yordamchi bo'lsa — kimning guruhida) |
| `GET /api/audit-logs?actorId={id}` | `audit.log.view` | Activity History (sahifalangan) — `actorId` filtri yo'q bo'lsa qo'shing |
- Overview: `TeacherOverviewDto` ga (yoki alohida `GET {id}/summary`) — filial(lar) nomi, ish boshlagan sana (`User.CreatedAt`), guruh/student soni, ixtisoslik (S05.02 dan keyin profildan). Portfolio hali bo'lmasa maydonlar `null`.
- Hisoblar `Api/Users/TeacherWorkloadQueries.cs` (sof so'rovlar, `TeachersController.Performance` dagi formulalar bilan **bir xil**: uni ham shundan foydalanadigan qiling — dublikat bo'lmasin).
- Sana oralig'i: standart oxirgi 30 kun; `to − from ≤ 366` (dashboarddagi qoida).

### 3.2. Frontend (`teachers/[id]/page.tsx`)
Tab tartibi: **Umumiy** · Guruhlar · Studentlar · Jadval · **Davomat** · **Homework** · **Imtihonlar** · **Yordamchilar** · Natijadorlik · **Faoliyat tarixi** (faqat `can("audit.log.view")`).
- Har tab alohida komponent (fayl ~700 qatorga o'smasin): `components/teachers/TeacherOverviewTab.tsx`, `TeacherAttendanceTab.tsx`, ... Ma'lumot `useQuery` bilan tab ochilganda yuklanadi (`enabled` — faol tabga qarab yoki komponent ichida).
- Davomat/Imtihon tablari: kichik jadval (guruh · qiymat · trend); grafik **yo'q** (recharts S05.03 da). Faoliyat tarixi: sahifalangan jadval (`Pagination`), amal · obyekt · vaqt (`lib/format-date.ts`).
- O'qituvchi o'z sahifasida Faoliyat tarixini ko'rmaydi (ruxsat yo'q) — tab yashiriladi.

## 4. Testlar — `TeacherWorkloadTests.cs` (~7)
1. Workload: 2 guruh, ma'lum davomat/homework/imtihon → aniq raqamlar. 2. Tekshirish o'rtacha vaqti (soat) to'g'ri. 3. Bekor qilingan dars davomat foiziga kirmaydi (S02.02 dan keyin).
4. Teacher boshqa o'qituvchining workload'iga → 403; o'zinikini → 200; administrator boshqa filial o'qituvchisiga → 403. 5. `support-teachers` ro'yxati. 6. `audit-logs?actorId=` faqat SA/AD; teacher → 403.
Mavjud `TeacherPerformanceTests` o'zgarishsiz yashil (formula umumiylashtirilsa ham).
