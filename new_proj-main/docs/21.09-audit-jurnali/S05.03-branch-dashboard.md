## 1. Maqsad
Foydalanuvchi talabi: "har bir filialga bosganda o'sha yerda nechta talaba, qanaqa daromad… o'zini dashboardi bo'lsin". Hozir `/branches/{id}` = umumiy dashboard'ning shu filialga qulflangan nusxasi: **Finance** to'liq, lekin TZ §5.2 ning **Academic, Teachers, CRM** bo'limlari yo'q.

## 2. Hozirgi holat (kodda tekshirilgan)
- `frontend/src/app/(admin)/branches/[id]/page.tsx` → `OrganizationDashboard` (`lockedBranchId`) — `components/dashboard/OrganizationDashboard.tsx`.
- Backend: `GET /api/dashboard/organization?from=&to=&branchId=` (`Api/Dashboard/DashboardController.cs`, `dashboard.view`; scope `AllOrganization`/`MyBranches`; davr ≤ 366 kun; `BranchClock` bilan filial vaqt zonasi; to'lovlar bir kun zaxira bilan olinib filial kalendari bo'yicha filtrlanadi). `OrganizationDashboardDto` (`DashboardDtos.cs`): studentlar, guruhlar, moliya, lid/trial/konversiya, davomat, retention.
- `Api/Finance/FinanceQueries.cs` — og'ir hisoblar uchun **SQL naqshi** (`FromSql`/`GROUP BY` bazada — LINQ xotirada emas).
- Grafik kutubxonasi **yo'q** (`frontend/package.json`); `@dnd-kit/core` bor. Hisoblarni Redis'siz xotira keshiga qo'yamiz (S09.07 da Redis'ga ko'chadi).
- Ma'lumot: `ExamResult` (S04.01 dan keyin Grammar/Vocabulary), `Attendance`, `HomeworkSubmission.SubmittedAt/ReviewedAt`, `Lead`, `TrialLesson`, `Invoice`, `SupportCase`, `Enrollment`.

## 3. Nima qilinadi

### 3.1. Backend — `GET /api/dashboard/branch/{id}?from=&to=` (`dashboard.view`, scope tekshiruvi `AllowedBranchIds`; filial scope'da bo'lmasa 403)
Bitta endpoint, to'rt blok (Finance mavjud `organization?branchId=` dan olinadi yoki shu yerga ko'chiriladi — takror hisoblamang):

| Blok | Ko'rsatkichlar (aniq ta'rif) |
|---|---|
| **Academic** | Listening/Reading/Writing/Speaking o'rtachalari (davrdagi `ExamResult` — faqat imtihon), Grammar %, Vocabulary % (`null` ni hisobga olmay), **oylik imtihon trendi** (`Overall` o'rtachasi, oxirgi 6 oy), davomat % (bekor qilingan darslarsiz) |
| **Teachers** | har o'qituvchi: o'tkazilgan darslar, guruh davomati %, homework tekshirish o'rtacha vaqti (soat), **retention** (davr boshida faol studentlarning qanchasi hali faol), ochiq/yopilgan support case'lar. Reyting jadvali (davomat bo'yicha) |
| **CRM** | lidlar, bog'lanilgan, trial band, trial keldi, **trial → student**, **trial → to'lov qildi**, yo'qotilgan, **manba bo'yicha taqsimot** (`Lead.Source` × holat) |
| **Finance** | mavjud: tushum, kutilayotgan, qarz, qarzdorlar, chegirma, qaytarilgan |
- CRM hisoblari uchun `Api/Crm/LeadFunnelQueries.cs` (sof so'rovlar; S06.02 shundan foydalanadi — shu yerda **to'g'ri va qayta ishlatiladigan** yozing).
- **Unumdorlik:** guruhlangan SQL, `from/to` bo'yicha; natija `IMemoryCache` da **5 daqiqa** (kalit: filial+davr+scope). 5000 talaba bilan `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` (dev bazada 180 — kam; kerak bo'lsa vaqtinchalik ko'p ma'lumot yaratib o'lchang va natijani `docs/09` ga yozing).
- Sana/vaqt zonasi: filial `Timezone` (`BranchClock`); to'lovlar/tushum mavjud usulda.

### 3.2. Frontend
- `npm install recharts` (SSR muammosi yo'q; faqat `"use client"` komponentlarda). Ranglar — `ink-*`/`brand-*` tokenlari (tungi rejimda ham o'qiladigan).
- `components/dashboard/BranchDashboard.tsx` (`branches/[id]/page.tsx` endi shuni ko'rsatadi): sarlavha (filial nomi, manzil, vaqt zonasi) + davr tanlash + `Tabs`: **Umumiy** (mavjud `OrganizationDashboard` kartalari), **O'quv** (o'rtacha ballar polosalari + trend chizig'i), **O'qituvchilar** (jadval), **CRM** (voronka + manba jadvali), **Moliya**.
- Har blok alohida `useQuery` emas — bitta so'rov, tab almashganda qayta yuklanmaydi. Bo'sh ma'lumot → `EmptyState`. Mobilda (390px) grafiklar yonma-yon emas, ustma-ust.
- Rolga ko'ra: administrator faqat o'z filialini, operator (`branch.view`) — faqat CRM va Umumiy (ruxsat: `dashboard.view` MyBranches — Finance/O'qituvchilar bloklarini `payments.view`/`users.view` bilan yashiring, backend ham shu bloklarni ruxsatsizga bo'sh qaytarsin).
