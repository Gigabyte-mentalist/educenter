## 1. Maqsad
TZ §11 lid maydonlarida **preferred time** bor, `Lead` da yo'q. TZ §12 (Group Recommendation) "student level", "preferred time", "schedule compatibility" omillarini talab qiladi — hozir faqat 4 omil ishlaydi (`docs/09` 1.15: kurs 50%, filial 25%, bo'sh o'rin 20%, guruh faolligi 5%).

## 2. Hozirgi holat (kodda tekshirilgan)
- `Modules.Crm/Domain/Lead.cs`: `FullName, Phone, Source, InterestedCourse (matn), OperatorId, Status, Notes, LostReason, ConvertedStudentId, Priority`. `PreferredTime` va `CurrentLevel` **yo'q**.
- `Api/Crm/LeadsController.cs`: `CreateLeadRequest(BranchId, FullName, Phone, Source, InterestedCourse)`; `GET {id}/group-recommendations` (ballash, `GroupRecommendationDto(... MatchPercent, Reasons, TeacherName, NextLessons)`); `PlacementAttempt` natijasi (`RecommendedLevel`) lidga **bog'lanmagan** — faqat konvertatsiya oynasida taklif.
- Guruhning haftalik jadvali **yo'q** (S07.04 da paydo bo'ladi) — "qulay vaqt" hozircha guruhning **haqiqiy yaqin darslari** (`NextLessons`, `Lesson.StartsAt` filial vaqt zonasida) bilan solishtiriladi.
- Kurs darajasi: `Course.LevelCategory` (matn: "IELTS", "General"…) va kurs/guruh nomlari ("General English A1", "IELTS Intermediate") — aniq daraja maydoni yo'q.
- Frontend: `/leads` (yaratish oynasi), `leads/[id]/page.tsx` (profil), `components/PlacementTestDrawer.tsx`, `components/crm/ConvertLeadDrawer.tsx`.

## 3. Nima qilinadi
- **Domen:** `Lead.PreferredTime` (`string?`, ≤100 — kodlar vergul bilan: `Morning`(8–12), `Afternoon`(12–17), `Evening`(17–21), `Weekend`; bo'sh = farqi yo'q; **massiv ustuni emas**, matn — EF quirk'idan qochish) va `Lead.CurrentLevel` (`string?`, ≤50). Metod `UpdatePreferences(...)`. Migratsiya **`AddLeadPreferences`**.
- **Backend:** `CreateLeadRequest`/`LeadDto` ga ikki maydon (ixtiyoriy); `PATCH /api/leads/{id}` (yo'q bo'lsa: `leads.edit` bilan `{ preferredTime?, currentLevel? }` — mavjud `PATCH {id}/status` ga tegmang).
  Daraja testi natijasi (`POST placement-test`) `Lead.CurrentLevel` ni **taklif** sifatida yangilamaydi — operator tasdiqlaydi (mavjud qaror); lekin lid kartasida "Oxirgi test: B1" ko'rinsin.
- **Ballash (yangi tavsiya formulasi)** — `group-recommendations` da 6 omil:
  | Omil | Og'irlik | Qanday |
  |---|---|---|
  | Qiziqqan kurs mosligi | 35% | mavjud |
  | **Daraja mosligi** | 20% | `LevelOrder = [A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, IELTS Foundation, IELTS Intermediate, IELTS Advanced]`; kurs/guruh nomidagi token indeksi bilan lid darajasi indeksi farqi 0 → 100%, 1 → 60%, ≥2 → 0; daraja noma'lum bo'lsa omil **hisoblanmaydi** va og'irlik qolganlarga taqsimlanadi (soxta ball yo'q) |
  | Filial | 15% | mavjud |
  | **Qulay vaqt** | 15% | guruhning `NextLessons` boshlanish soati/hafta kuni lid `PreferredTime` bucket'lariga tushsa 100%, aks holda 0; vaqt ko'rsatilmagan lid → hisoblanmaydi |
  | Bo'sh o'rin | 10% | mavjud (nisbatan) |
  | Guruh faolligi | 5% | mavjud |
  `Reasons` ro'yxatiga yangilari: "Daraja mos (B1)", "Qulay vaqtda (kechqurun)". Omillar hisoblanmagan bo'lsa ball **qayta normallashtiriladi** (yig'indi 100%).
- **Frontend:** lid yaratish oynasi va profilida "Qulay vaqt" (checkbox guruhi) va "Daraja" (Input + `PlacementTestDrawer` natijasi "qo'llash"); `TrialDrawer`/`ConvertLeadDrawer` dagi tavsiya kartalarida yangi sabablar avtomatik ko'rinadi.

## 4. Testlar — `GroupRecommendationTests.cs` ga +~5
1. Darajasi mos guruh yuqori ball, 2 pog'ona farq — past. 2. Qulay vaqtga tushgan guruh yuqori. 3. Daraja va vaqt bo'sh → eski xatti-harakat (mavjud 3 test o'zgarishsiz yashil, foizlar qayta normallashtirilgani uchun **kutilgan raqamlar o'zgarsa** testni yangilang va sababini izohlang).
4. `PATCH` lid — boshqa filial lidiga 403. 5. Ball 0–100 oralig'ida, yig'indi to'g'ri.
