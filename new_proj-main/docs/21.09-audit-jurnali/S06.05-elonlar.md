## 1. Maqsad
Ota-ona va studentlar markaz e'lonlarini (bayram, jadval, yangi kurs) ko'rsin. Hozir `Announcements` faqat **xodimlar chat kanali** (`StaffChannelKind.Announcements`) — student/ota-ona ko'rmaydi.

## 2. Hozirgi holat
- `Modules.Chat/Domain/ChatRoom.cs`: `StaffChannelKind { General, Teachers, IeltsTeachers, Administrators, Announcements }` — faqat xodimlar (`staff.channels.access`).
- E'lon entity/endpoint **yo'q**. Student/ota-ona/o'qituvchi dashboardlarida e'lon bloki yo'q (`Api/Dashboard/DashboardDtos.cs`).
- Bildirishnoma: `NotificationDispatcher.NotifyAsync(orgId, userIds, msg)` — katta auditoriya uchun `dedupeKey` mavjudlarini yuklaydi; minglab foydalanuvchiga **partiyalab** yuboring.

## 3. Nima qilinadi
- **Domen** (migratsiya **`AddAnnouncements`**): `Announcement(OrganizationId, BranchId?, GroupId?, Title (≤200), Body (≤4000), Audience {All, Students, Parents, Staff}, AuthorId, PublishedAt, ExpiresAt?)`. `BranchId`/`GroupId` ikkalasi bo'sh = butun tashkilot; `GroupId` berilsa filial guruhdan olinadi.
- **Ruxsatlar** (yangi): `announcements.view` — hamma rol (Student `Self`, Parent `MyChildren`, Teacher `MyGroups`, Support `AssignedGroups`, Operator/AD `MyBranches`, SA `AllOrganization`); `announcements.manage` — SA `AllOrganization`, AD `MyBranches` (o'z filiali/guruhi).
- **Ko'rinish qoidasi** (`AnnouncementQueries.VisibleTo(scope, roleAudience)`): student — o'z faol guruhlari/filiali/tashkilot e'lonlari, `Audience ∈ {All, Students}`; ota-ona — farzandlarining shular, `Audience ∈ {All, Parents}`; xodim — filiali, `Audience ∈ {All, Staff}`; muddati o'tganlar yashirin. Auditoriyani rol bo'yicha aniqlash uchun **rol nomi emas** — `announcements.view` scope turi (`Self`→Students, `MyChildren`→Parents, qolgani→Staff) yoki foydalanuvchi rol kodlari `NotificationCatalog.ForRoles` naqshida (mavjud usulni tekshiring).
- **Endpointlar:** `GET /api/announcements` (sahifalangan, faqat ko'rinadiganlar), `POST` (`announcements.manage`; scope: administrator faqat o'z filiali/guruhiga), `PATCH {id}`, `DELETE {id}`.
- **Bildirishnoma:** chiqarilganda `NotificationType.Announcement` auditoriyaga (`dedupeKey = "announcement:{id}"`), **partiyalar** (masalan 500 tadan) va katta auditoriyada fon ishi; S06.03 bor bo'lsa Email/SMS ham sozlamaga qarab.
- **Frontend:** `/announcements` sahifasi (ro'yxat; boshqaruvchi uchun "Yangi e'lon" drawer: sarlavha, matn, kimga, qayerga, muddat); student/ota-ona/o'qituvchi dashboardiga "So'nggi e'lonlar" kartasi (3 ta); nav: barcha rollarga "E'lonlar" (`lib/nav.ts` — `EVERYONE` emas, `ACADEMIC` + xodimlar).

## 4. Testlar — `AnnouncementTests.cs` (~8)
1. Administrator o'z filialiga e'lon beradi; shu filial studenti ko'radi, boshqa filial studenti ko'rmaydi. 2. Guruh e'loni faqat shu guruh a'zolariga. 3. `Audience = Parents`: ota-ona ko'radi, student ko'rmaydi.
4. Muddati o'tgan yashirin. 5. Teacher/student/parent `POST` → 403; administrator boshqa filialga → 403. 6. Bildirishnoma auditoriyaga bir marta (dedupe). 7. Sahifalash.
