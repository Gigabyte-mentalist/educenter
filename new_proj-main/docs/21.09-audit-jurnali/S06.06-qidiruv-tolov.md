## 1. Maqsad
TZ §27.3 `Ctrl+K` qidiruvi 9 tur: student, teacher, group, lead, **payment**, course, branch, ticket, **action**. Hozir 7 tasi bor (student, teacher, group, course, lead, branch, ticket). Yetishmaydi: **to'lov** va **amallar**.

## 2. Hozirgi holat (kodda tekshirilgan)
- `Api/Search/SearchController.cs` (`GET /api/search?q=`): har tur o'z ekrani ruxsati va scope bilan tekshiriladi (`PermissionResolver.ResolveAsync` to'g'ridan-to'g'ri — bitta endpoint-bitta policy emas); ruxsati yo'q tur natijada **umuman ko'rinmaydi**; `q` 2 belgidan qisqa → bo'sh. Natija `SearchResultDto(Type, Id, Title, Subtitle, Href)`; ba'zi turlar `?highlight=<id>` bilan yo'naltiradi (`use-highlight-target.ts`).
- `Modules.Finance/Domain/Payment.cs`: `InvoiceId, StudentId, Amount, RefundedAmount, Method, PaidAt, ReceivedBy, Note, IsVoided…` — **kvitansiya raqami maydoni yo'q** (qidiruv shu ustunlar bo'yicha).
- Frontend: `components/CommandPalette.tsx` (navigatsiya ro'yxati + backend natijalari, 250 ms debounce, tur ikonkalari), `lib/api/search.ts`.
- Testlar: `SearchTests.cs` (7 ta; namuna).

## 3. Nima qilinadi
- **`payment` turi** (`payments.view` scope bilan): qidiruv matni **summa** (raqamga o'giriladigan bo'lsa, probel/vergul olib tashlanib, aniq mos), **student ismi** yoki **izoh** (`Note`) bo'yicha; natija: `Title = "500 000 so'm — Student Ismi"` (`formatMoney` logikasi serverda: probel bilan guruhlash), `Subtitle = "18-sentabr · Naqd"` yoki bekor qilingan bo'lsa "Bekor qilingan"; `Href = /finance?highlight={paymentId}` (moliya sahifasi `highlight` ni to'lovlar tabida ochishi kerak — `finance/page.tsx` ni tekshiring; qo'llamasa `useHighlightTarget` ni ulang). Scope: `FinanceQueries` dagi filial filtri (to'lov `BranchId` ga ega) — administrator faqat o'z filiali to'lovlarini topadi; operator (`payments.view` yo'q) — natija turi ko'rinmaydi.
- **`action` turi — faqat frontend** (`CommandPalette.tsx`): ruxsat bilan (`usePermissions().can(...)`) shartlangan amallar ro'yxati, qidiruv matniga mos: "Yangi student" (`students.create` → `/students?new=1` yoki drawer'ni ochish), "Yangi lid" (`leads.create`), "Dars qo'shish" (`schedule.edit`), "To'lov qabul qilish" (`payments.create`), "Yangi guruh" (`groups.create`). Bosilganda tegishli sahifaga o'tib, mos drawer'ni ochadigan `?new=1` konventsiyasi (sahifalar `useSearchParams` ni o'qiydi; **yangi backend kerak emas**).
- Natija turi yorlig'i: "To'lov", "Amal" (`CommandPalette` dagi `TYPE_LABEL`).

## 4. Testlar — `SearchTests.cs` ga +~4
