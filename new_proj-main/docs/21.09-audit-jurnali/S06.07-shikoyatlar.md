## 1. Maqsad
TZ §41: `Complaint → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved → Feedback`, shikoyat CRM timeline'da saqlanadi. **`SupportCase` bu emas** (u o'qishda qiynalayotgan student signali — Support Needed Engine), shikoyat — mijozning noroziligi.

## 2. Hozirgi holat
Shikoyat entity/endpoint/ekran **yo'q**. Naqsh uchun eng yaqin: `Modules.Students/Domain/SupportCase.cs` + `Api/Support/SupportCasesController.cs` (holatlar, `assign`, `resolve`, timeline, bildirishnoma, `/support` sahifasi tab'lar bilan: Ko'rib chiqish / Jarayonda / Yopilgan, `?highlight=`).
Timeline: `TimelineEventType` (student uchun). Bildirishnoma: `NotificationRecipients.BranchAdministratorsAsync`.

## 3. Nima qilinadi
- **Domen** (migratsiya **`AddComplaints`**): `Complaint(OrganizationId, BranchId, StudentId?, AuthorUserId, Subject (≤200), Body (≤4000), Status {New, Assigned, InProgress, Resolved, Closed}, AssignedToUserId?, Resolution? (≤2000), ResolvedAt?, SatisfactionScore? (0–10), FeedbackComment?)`. Holat o'tishlari faqat oldinga (`New→Assigned→InProgress→Resolved→Closed`); `Resolved` dan keyin muallif baho qo'yadi → `Closed`.
- **Ruxsatlar:** `complaints.create` (Student `Self`, Parent `MyChildren`, Teacher `MyGroups`, Operator/AD `MyBranches`), `complaints.view` (SA `AllOrganization`, AD/OP `MyBranches`; muallif o'zinikini har doim ko'radi), `complaints.manage` (SA, AD — biriktirish, holat, yechim).
- **Endpointlar:** `GET /api/complaints` (sahifalangan; `status`, `search`; muallif o'zinikini, xodim scope'dagilarni), `POST` (filial: studentning faol guruhi filialidan yoki `branchId`; ota-ona farzandi uchun `studentId`), `POST {id}/assign` (`{ userId }` — filial xodimi), `POST {id}/status`, `POST {id}/resolve` (`{ resolution }` majburiy), `POST {id}/feedback` (faqat muallif, `Resolved` da: `{ score 0–10, comment? }`).
- **Bildirishnoma:** yangi shikoyat → filial administratorlariga; biriktirilganda ijrochiga; yechilganda muallifga ("bahoing kerak"). **Timeline:** `studentId` bor bo'lsa `COMPLAINT_CREATED`, `COMPLAINT_RESOLVED` (student timeline — TZ "CRM timeline'da saqlanadi"), `EVENT_CONFIG` ga qo'shing. Audit: `Complaint` `AuditedTypes` ga.
- **Frontend:** `/complaints` (`/support` sahifasi naqshi: tab'lar Yangi / Jarayonda / Yopilgan, ro'yxat, detal drawer, biriktirish/yechish); student/ota-ona/o'qituvchi uchun "Shikoyat yuborish" tugmasi (profil menyusi yoki dashboard) va "Mening shikoyatlarim"; `lib/nav.ts` ga xodimlar uchun havola.

## 4. Testlar — `ComplaintTests.cs` (~8)
1. Ota-ona shikoyat yuboradi → administrator ko'radi (boshqa filial administratori ko'rmaydi). 2. Biriktirish → ijrochiga bildirishnoma. 3. Holat orqaga qaytmaydi (409). 4. `resolve` yechimsiz → 400.
5. Faqat muallif baho qo'ya oladi; `Resolved` dan oldin → 409; baho 0–10 dan tashqari → 400. 6. Muallif faqat o'zinikini ko'radi (`GET` ro'yxat). 7. Teacher `manage` → 403. 8. Timeline hodisalari.
