## 1. Maqsad
Invoice oyma-oy avtomatik chiqadi (`BillingRules`); "shu oylik to'lovni ikki-uch qismga bo'lib, har biriga o'z muddati bilan to'lash" rejasi modellanmagan. TZ §25 "payment schedule".

## 2. Hozirgi holat (kodda tekshirilgan)
- `Modules.Finance/Domain/Invoice.cs`: `StudentId, EnrollmentId, GroupId, Description, PeriodStart, PeriodEnd, DueDate, Amount, DiscountAmount, CreditedAmount, PaidAmount, Currency, Status` (`Pending, PartiallyPaid, Paid, Cancelled` — "muddati o'tgan" alohida holat **emas**, `DueDate` bo'yicha hisoblanadi: `IsOverdue`), `CancelReason…`; to'lov `Payment(InvoiceId, Amount, …)` — **qisman to'lov allaqachon bor** (`PartiallyPaid`).
- `Api/Finance/FinanceController.cs`: `payments`, `void`, `cancel`, `advance-invoices`; `FinanceAdjustmentsController`: chegirma, promo, refund. `FinanceQueries.OpenOverdueAsync` — "muddati o'tgan" filial vaqt zonasi bo'yicha SQL'da; qarzdorlar `GROUP BY student`.
- `Api/Automation/BillingRules.cs`: faqat `Active` enrollment'ga oylik invoice; `PaymentReminders` (`DueDate` bo'yicha eslatma), `DebtFollowUp`. Bo'lingan qismlar shu mantiqlarga **tabiiy** kirishi kerak (har qism o'z `DueDate` iga ega invoice).

## 3. Nima qilinadi
- **Domen** (migratsiya **`AddInvoiceInstallments`**): `Invoice.ParentInvoiceId` (`Guid?`), `InstallmentNumber` (`int?`), `InstallmentCount` (`int?`). Bo'lingan asosiy invoice `Cancelled` emas — yangi **`Split`** holati (`InvoiceStatus` ga a'zo qo'shish — matn sifatida saqlanadi, migratsiya kerak emas) va hisoblarda **hisobga olinmaydi** (chunki qismlar summasi = asos summasi; ikki marta sanalmasin).
- **`POST /api/finance/invoices/{id}/split`** (`payments.edit`) `{ parts: [{ amount, dueDate }] }`: faqat **to'lanmagan/qisman to'lanmagan** (`PaidAmount == 0`) va `Split` emas invoice; `Σ amount == net (Amount − chegirma − kredit)` (aniq, tiyingacha — `decimal`), kamida 2 qism, `dueDate` lar o'sib boruvchi va `≥ PeriodStart`. Yangi invoice'lar (`ParentInvoiceId`, `InstallmentNumber/Count`, `Description = "… (2/3)"`) yaratiladi, asos `Split`. Timeline `INVOICE_SPLIT`? — **yo'q**, `DISCOUNT_APPLIED` kabi moliya hodisalari uchun mavjud naqshni ko'ring va bitta hodisa qo'shing; audit avtomatik (`Invoice`).
- **Ta'sir tekshiruvi (majburiy):** `BillingRules` bir enrollment/davr uchun ikkinchi invoice yaratmasin (`Split` asosi mavjud deb hisoblansin); qarzdorlar/`overview`/dashboard `Split` invoice'ni qarzga qo'shmasin (yalpi jamlanmasin); `FinanceReportsController.Summary` "billed" hisobi qismlarni sanasin, asosni emas; eslatmalar har qism `DueDate` iga qarab.
- **Bekor qilish:** qismni alohida `cancel` qilish mumkin; **hammasini** qaytarish (`unsplit`) — hech qism to'lanmagan bo'lsa — ixtiyoriy, birinchi versiyada **yo'q** (foydalanuvchi qismlarni bekor qilib yangi invoice yozadi).
- **Frontend:** `components/Finance.tsx` da invoice qatorida "Bo'lish" (faqat `can("payments.edit")` va to'lanmagan) → drawer: qismlar soni (2–6), har biriga summa (avtomatik teng bo'lish, oxirgi qism farqni oladi) va sana; qism qatorlari "2/3" belgisi bilan guruhlanadi.
