## 1. Maqsad
Barcha fayllar `App_Data/` da (lokal disk, docker volume `educenter-app-data`) — API'ni bir nechta nusxada ishga tushirib bo'lmaydi, backup alohida qiyin.

## 2. Hozirgi holat (kodda tekshirilgan)
Har biri o'z papkasi va o'z `*FileStorage` klassi bilan: `Api/Chat/ChatFileStorage.cs`, `Api/Lms/ResourceFileStorage.cs`, `Api/Students/StudentDocumentFileStorage.cs`, `Api/Ai/Knowledge/KnowledgeFileStorage.cs`, `Api/Ai/Speaking/SpeakingAudioStorage.cs` (+ S03.03 homework, S04.04 user-photo, S05.02 teacher-credentials, S07.01 org-logo bo'lsa). Har birida bir xil shakl: `Validate(IFormFile)`, `SaveAsync` (tasodifiy kalit), `OpenAsync`/`Delete`. `IWebHostEnvironment.ContentRootPath` asosida `App_Data/...`.

## 3. Nima qilinadi
- **`IFileStore`** (`Api/Files/`): `Task<string> SaveAsync(string bucket, Stream content, string contentType, CancellationToken ct)` (kalitni o'zi yaratadi, `bucket` — "student-documents" kabi), `Task<Stream?> OpenAsync(string bucket, string key, CancellationToken ct)`, `Task DeleteAsync(string bucket, string key, CancellationToken ct)`, `Task<bool> ExistsAsync(...)`.
- **`LocalFileStore`** — hozirgi xatti-harakatni saqlaydi (`App_Data/<bucket>/<key>`), **standart (Development)**.
- **`S3FileStore`** — `AWSSDK.S3` (MinIO ham shu SDK bilan, `ServiceURL` + `ForcePathStyle = true`). Sozlama `Files:Store` (`Provider: local|s3`, `S3: { ServiceUrl, Bucket, AccessKey, SecretKey, Region, ForcePathStyle }`) — **kalitlar faqat env/`appsettings.Local.json`**.
- Har bir mavjud `*FileStorage` klassi **`IFileStore` ni chaqiradigan** ingichka qatlamga aylanadi — validatsiya/nomlash mantig'i (kengaytma oq ro'yxati, hajm chegarasi, skaner — S08.03) **o'zgarmaydi**, faqat saqlash joyi `IFileStore` orqali. Har biri o'z `bucket` nomini beradi.
- `docker-compose.yml` ga `minio` (`minio/minio`, konsol porti debug uchun, **boshqa loyihalar bilan to'qnashmaydigan** nom/portlar; `lcsystem-minio-1` allaqachon ishlab turibdi bu mashinada — **shu konteynerga tegmang**, o'z nomingiz bilan yangisini oching yoki dev uchun local qoldiring va faqat production konfiguratsiyasini yozing).
- **Ko'chirish skripti:** `dotnet EduCenter.Api.dll migrate-files --from local --to s3` (S09.01 dagi buyruqlar naqshi) — `App_Data` dagi har faylni tegishli bucket'ga yuklaydi; **bazadagi kalitlar o'zgarmaydi** (nom sxemasi bir xil saqlansin), shuning uchun ko'chirish bir martalik va qaytarib bo'ladigan.
- **Fayl xizmat qilish:** hozir ham bearer-token endpoint orqali (`GET .../file`) — `IFileStore.OpenAsync` dan oqim; **to'g'ridan-to'g'ri S3 pre-signed URL** bu qadamda **yo'q** (keyingi iteratsiya — ruxsat tekshiruvi hozirgi kabi serverda qoladi, soddaroq va xavfsizroq).

## 4. Testlar
- `FileStoreTests.cs` (~6, `LocalFileStore` va **`MinioFileStore` — agar CI'da MinIO konteyner mavjud bo'lsa**, aks holda faqat `LocalFileStore` + interfeys shartnoma testi): saqlash/o'qish/o'chirish/mavjudlik; ikki bucket bir-biriga aralashmaydi; noto'g'ri kalit → `null`/404.
- Mavjud yuklash testlarining **hammasi** (`StudentDocumentsTests`, `ChatTests` fayl qismi, `KnowledgeAndTutorTests`, va S03.03/S04.04/S05.02/S07.01 bo'lsa ularniki) `LocalFileStore` bilan yashil qolishi shart — bu regressiya nazorati.

## 5. Boshlashdan oldin o'qing
Yuqoridagi barcha `*FileStorage.cs` fayllar (faqat metod imzolari — dublikat qilmang, umumlashtiring), `Program.cs` (fayl servislari ro'yxati — hammasini `IFileStore` ga o'tkazasiz), `docker-compose.yml`.
