## 1. Maqsad
TZ §6: 7 mezonli KPI (Student Progress 30%, Attendance 15%, Homework Completion 15%, Punctuality 10%, Feedback Speed 10%, Retention 10%, Student/Parent Feedback 10%), Super Admin og'irliklarni sozlaydi. Hozir faqat `TeacherPerformanceDto` (4 raqam, TZ modeli emas — ataylab, `TeachersController.Performance` sinf izohida yozilgan).

## 2. Hozirgi holat
`Api/Users/TeachersController.cs` `Performance` — `ActiveStudents, GroupsCount, AttendanceRate, HomeworkCompletionRate, AverageExamScore`. S05.01 dan keyin `Api/Users/TeacherWorkloadQueries.cs` bor (davomat/homework/imtihon/retention hisoblari). **Punctuality kuzatilmaydi** — `Lesson` da o'qituvchi "keldi/kelmadi" belgisi yo'q (faqat student davomati bor).

## 3. Nima qilinadi
- **Domen:** `Lesson.TeacherAttendanceMarkedAt` (`DateTimeOffset?`) — o'qituvchi davomat saqlaganda avtomatik to'ldiriladi (`AttendanceController.Mark` ichida); punctuality = `TeacherAttendanceMarkedAt` dars boshlanish vaqtidan qancha keyin/oldin (masalan darsdan 10 daqiqa ichida belgilasa 100%). Bu **taxminiy vakolat** — haqiqiy "kelish vaqti" emas, izohda ochiq yozing. `KpiWeights(OrganizationId, Metric (string), Weight (int, 0-100))`. Migratsiya **`AddKpiWeights`** (+ `AddTeacherPunctualityTracking`, birga qo'shilishi mumkin).
- **Hisoblash** (`Api/Users/TeacherKpiCalculator.cs`, sof funksiyalar — S04.02 `LearningProfileCalculator` naqshi):
  | Mezon | Manba |
  |---|---|
  | Student Progress | S04.02 `LearningProfileCalculator` — o'qituvchi guruhlaridagi studentlar `Overall` trendi (o'sish %) |
  | Attendance | `TeacherWorkloadQueries` (S05.01) |
  | Homework Completion | shundan |
  | Punctuality | yuqoridagi yangi ustun |
  | Feedback Speed | homework tekshirish o'rtacha vaqti (S05.01) — teskari me'yorlangan (tezroq = yuqori ball) |
  | Retention | shundan |
  | Student/Parent Feedback | **S10.02 tugagunga qadar `null`** — og'irlik qolganlarga qayta taqsimlanadi (S04.02 dagi "ma'lumot yo'q → qayta normallash" qoidasi) |
- **Endpointlar:** `GET /api/teachers/{id}/kpi?from=&to=` (`kpi.view` — yangi: SA `AllOrganization`, AD `MyBranches`, Teacher `Self`), `GET/PUT /api/settings/kpi-weights` (`kpi.weights.edit` — yangi: SA). Og'irliklar yig'indisi 100 ± 1 (yaxlitlash xatosi) bo'lmasa 400.
- **Frontend:** Teacher 360° (S05.01) ga **"KPI"** tabi — radar/polosa ko'rinish, har mezon foizi va vazni; `/settings/kpi` — og'irliklarni sozlash formasi (slayderlar, yig'indi ko'rsatkichi). Org dashboard (S05.04-A) ga `teacherKpiAverage`.

## 4. Testlar — `TeacherKpiTests.cs` (~8)
1. Ma'lum ma'lumot bilan aniq KPI ball. 2. Og'irlik o'zgarsa natija o'zgaradi. 3. Feedback yo'q → qolgan 6 mezon qayta normallanadi (yig'indi 100%). 4. Yig'indi ≠ 100 → 400. 5. Teacher o'zinikini, boshqasini emas; administrator o'z filiali. 6. Punctuality: darsdan 5 daqiqa keyin belgilangan → yuqori ball; 2 soat keyin → past.
