## 1. Maqsad

## 2. Hozirgi holat
`Modules.Automation/Domain/AutomationRule.cs` (`AutomationTrigger.Sweep` naqshi — S04.05 dagi kabi yangi trigger qo'shish mumkin), `Api/Automation/AutomationCatalog.cs`. S06.07 `Complaint.SatisfactionScore` — alohida oqim, aralashtirmang.

## 3. Nima qilinadi
- **Domen** (migratsiya **`AddFeedback`**): `FeedbackSurvey(OrganizationId, BranchId?, TeacherId?, CourseId?, StudentId, Trigger {CourseCompleted, Quarterly, SupportClosed}, RequestedAt, RespondedAt?)`, `FeedbackResponse` (`SurveyId`, `Score` 0–10, `Comment?`). Bitta `FeedbackSurvey` = bitta so'rov, `FeedbackResponse` javob kelganda to'ldiriladi (1:1 — alohida jadvalga ehtiyoj yo'q, `FeedbackSurvey` ga `Score`/`Comment` qo'shing, soddalik uchun).
- **Yaratilish:** `AutomationCatalog` ga yangi qoida `FeedbackRequest` (parametrlar: `TriggerDaysAfterCourseEnd`, standart amallar: so'rov yuborish) — S04.05 naqshi; kurs tugaganda (guruh `Completed` — S02.01) yoki har chorakda (`Sweep`) yoki support case `Resolved` bo'lganda (S06.07 bilan **aralashtirmang** — u alohida `SatisfactionScore`).
- **Endpointlar:** `GET /api/feedback/pending` (`feedback.submit` — Student `Self`, Parent `MyChildren`: javobsiz so'rovlar), `POST /api/feedback/{id}/respond` `{ score, comment? }`, `GET /api/feedback/summary?branchId=&teacherId=&courseId=&from=&to=` (`feedback.view` — SA `AllOrganization`, AD `MyBranches`): NPS = promoter% (9–10) − detractor% (0–6), o'rtacha ball, javob darajasi.
- **Frontend:** student/ota-ona dashboardida "So'nggi tajribangiz qanday?" karta (0–10 tugmalar); `/settings/feedback` yoki filial dashboardi (S05.03) ga NPS bloki qo'shiladi.
- S10.01 KPI: "Student/Parent Feedback" mezoni endi `FeedbackSurvey.Score` (o'qituvchiga bog'liq bo'lganlar) o'rtachasidan hisoblanadi.

## 4. Testlar — `FeedbackTests.cs` (~7)
1. So'rov yaratiladi (kurs tugaganda), student javob beradi. 2. NPS hisobi to'g'ri (aniq promoter/detractor sonlari bilan). 3. Filial/o'qituvchi/kurs bo'yicha filtr. 4. Teacher/operator `summary` → 403. 5. Javobsiz so'rovlar ro'yxati. 6. Bitta so'rovga ikkinchi marta javob → 409/e'tiborsiz.
