## 1. Maqsad
TZ §26: "platforma oddiy admin panel ko'rinishida bo'lmasin" — student uchun XP, Level, Streak, Badges, Achievements. Bu qadam **asos**: XP hisoblash va ko'rsatish. Challenge/leaderboard S10.05, o'yinlar S10.06.

## 2. Hozirgi holat
XP/badge/streak — hech qanday izi yo'q. Manba bo'ladigan hodisalar allaqachon bor: `Attendance` (dars kelishi), `HomeworkSubmission` (o'z vaqtida topshirish — `SubmittedAt <= Homework.Deadline`), `WritingEvaluation`/`SpeakingSession` (AI mashqi), `ExamResult` (imtihon natijasi). Timeline naqshi (`StudentTimelineEvent`) — XP hodisasi ham shunga o'xshash yoziladi.

## 3. Nima qilinadi
- **Domen** (migratsiya **`AddGamification`**): `XpEvent(OrganizationId, StudentId, Source {LessonAttended, HomeworkOnTime, WritingCompleted, SpeakingCompleted, ExamPassed, StreakBonus}, Points, OccurredAt, ReferenceId?)` — **har hodisa alohida qator** (jamlanmagan — badge/streak hisoblashda tarix kerak). `StudentXp(StudentId, TotalPoints, Level, CurrentStreakDays, LongestStreakDays, LastActiveDate)` — jamlanma, tez o'qish uchun. `Badge(Code, Name, Description, IconKey, Criteria (JSON yoki kod))`, `StudentBadge(StudentId, BadgeId, EarnedAt)`.
- **XP manbalari va ballari** (kod konstantasi, keyinchalik sozlanadigan — birinchi versiyada qattiq): dars kelishi +5, homework o'z vaqtida +10, kechikib +5, Writing/Speaking mashqi tugatish +15, imtihon ≥ 70% +20. **Bir xil hodisa uchun ikki marta XP berilmasin** (`ReferenceId` unique index `(StudentId, Source, ReferenceId)`).
- **Yozish nuqtasi:** yangi hodisa yaratilgan joylarning **har birida** (`AttendanceController.Mark` — Present bo'lsa, `HomeworkController.Submit`, `WritingEvaluator` tugallanganda, `SpeakingService.FinishAsync`, `ExamsController.RecordResult`) `XpService.AwardAsync(studentId, source, points, referenceId)` chaqiriladi — **hodisa nuqtalarini to'liq ro'yxatlang va har birini alohida commit/testda tekshiring** (S03.04 dagi "7 joy" xavfiga o'xshash — unutish oson).
- **Daraja:** XP egri chizig'i (masalan `Level = floor(sqrt(TotalPoints / 100))`) — formula kod konstantasi, `XpService.LevelFor(points)`.
- **Streak:** kunlik faollik (kamida bitta XP hodisasi) — `LastActiveDate` bilan solishtirib oshiriladi/nolga tushadi (kunlik `Sweep` yoki hodisa yozilganda hisoblanadi — hodisa nuqtasida hisoblash soddaroq, alohida job kerak emas).
- **Badge mezonlari** (kod: `IBadgeRule.Check(StudentXp, IReadOnlyList<XpEvent>) => bool`), misollar: "10 kun ketma-ket faollik", "50 homework o'z vaqtida", "Birinchi Writing 7.0+". Yangi XP hodisasi yozilganda mos qoidalar tekshiriladi, yangi badge bo'lsa `StudentBadge` + bildirishnoma.
- **Ruxsat:** `gamification.view` — Student `Self`, Parent `MyChildren`, Teacher `MyGroups` (rag'batlantirish uchun ko'rish, tahrirlash emas). Standart holatda **hech kim boshqasining XP/badge'ini o'zgartira olmaydi** — bu faqat tizim hisoblaydi.
- **Frontend:** student dashboardiga XP/daraja/streak karta (progress bar), "Yutuqlarim" sahifasi (badge grid). Ota-ona farzandining darajasini ko'radi (raqobat emas — qo'llab-quvvatlash konteksti).

## 4. Testlar — `GamificationTests.cs` (~10)
1. Har hodisa nuqtasi (5 ta) to'g'ri XP beradi. 2. Bir xil hodisa ikki marta yozilsa XP takrorlanmaydi. 3. Daraja formulasi. 4. Streak: ketma-ket kunlar oshadi, bir kun o'tkazib yuborilsa nolga tushadi. 5. Badge mezoni bajarilganda beriladi va takrorlanmaydi. 6. Ota-ona/teacher faqat ko'radi, o'zgartira olmaydi (endpoint yo'q — bu tabiiy).
