## 1. Maqsad
TZ §26: Daily/Weekly Challenges, Leaderboard (**"optional"** — TZ o'zi shunday belgilagan). S10.04 dagi XP asosida qo'shimcha maqsadlar va (ixtiyoriy, standart o'chiq) reyting.

## 2. Hozirgi holat
S10.04: `XpEvent`, `StudentXp`, `XpService`. S04.05 `AutomationCatalog` naqshi (sozlanadigan qoidalar, `Sweep` trigger).

## 3. Nima qilinadi
- **Domen** (migratsiya **`AddGamificationChallenges`**): `DailyChallenge(OrganizationId, Date, Description, XpReward, Criteria {LessonsAttended, HomeworkSubmitted, AiPracticeCompleted} — kod enum)`, `WeeklyChallenge` (o'xshash, `WeekStart`), `StudentChallengeProgress(StudentId, ChallengeId, ChallengeType, Progress, CompletedAt?)`.
- **Generatsiya:** `AutomationSweepJob` (S04.05 naqshi) har kuni/hafta boshida shablonlardan tanlab yangi challenge yaratadi (kod ichidagi shablon ro'yxati — "3 ta darsga kel", "5 ta homework topshir", "2 ta AI mashq qil"). Tashkilot buni o'chirishi mumkin (`AutomationSettings`).
- **Progress:** S10.04 dagi hodisa nuqtalarida (`XpService.AwardAsync` ichida yoki undan keyin) faol challenge'lar progressi yangilanadi; bajarilganda `XpEvent(Source: ChallengeCompleted)` + bildirishnoma.
- **Leaderboard (standart o'chiq — maxfiylik!):** tashkilot sozlamasi (S07.01) `Gamification:LeaderboardEnabled` (standart `false`). Yoqilsa: `GET /api/gamification/leaderboard?groupId=` — **faqat guruh ichida** (butun tashkilot emas — kichik markazda ham noqulay raqobat), ism o'rniga **taxallus** (`StudentXp.DisplayName?` — student o'zi tanlaydi, bo'sh bo'lsa "Anonim N"). Ruxsat `gamification.leaderboard.view` — Student `MyGroups`(o'z guruhi doskasi)/Self kabi emas, aniqrog'i guruh a'zolariga.
- **Frontend:** dashboardda "Bugungi maqsad" / "Haftalik maqsad" karta (progress bar); leaderboard yoqilgan bo'lsa `/leaderboard` sahifasi (guruh tanlash, taxallus sozlash).
