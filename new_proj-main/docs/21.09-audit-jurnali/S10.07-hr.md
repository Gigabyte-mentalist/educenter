## 1. Maqsad
TZ §43 (Faza 6, "keyingi bosqich" deb TZ'ning o'zida belgilangan): shtat, shartnoma, ish yuklamasi, davomat, ta'til, ish haqi.

## 2. Hozirgi holat
Xodim = `User` + rol. Shartnoma/ish haqi/ta'til — hech narsa yo'q. S10.01 dan `Lesson.TeacherAttendanceMarkedAt` bor (punctuality uchun) — bu `StaffAttendance` bilan **bir xil narsa emas** (u faqat dars belgilash vaqti, umumiy ish davomati emas).

## 3. Nima qilinadi (eng katta qadam — ichki bosqichlarga bo'lib boring)

### 3.1. Shtat va shartnoma (migratsiya **`AddHrEmployment`**)
`Employment(UserId, BranchId, Position, EmploymentType {FullTime, PartTime, Contractor}, StartDate, EndDate?, ContractFileKey?)` — bir foydalanuvchida bir nechta (filial/lavozim tarixi). Fayl yuklash — S08.03/S09.06 naqshi.

### 3.2. Ish yuklamasi va davomat
`StaffAttendance(UserId, Date, CheckIn?, CheckOut?, Status {Present, Absent, Leave, Holiday})` — qo'lda kiritiladigan (avtomatik kuzatish infratuzilmasi yo'q; kelajakda `Lesson.TeacherAttendanceMarkedAt` bilan boyitilishi mumkin, bu qadamda **qo'lda**). `POST /api/hr/attendance` (`hr.manage`) — kun/xodim bo'yicha belgilash (davomat sahifasi naqshi — `AttendanceController.Mark` shakli).

### 3.3. Ta'til
`LeaveRequest(UserId, Type {Annual, Sick, Unpaid}, FromDate, ToDate, Reason, Status {Pending, Approved, Rejected}, DecidedBy?, DecidedAt?)`. `POST /api/hr/leave-requests` (xodim o'zi, `hr.view` Self orqali — yoki yangi `hr.leave.request`), `POST {id}/approve|reject` (`hr.manage`).

### 3.4. Ish haqi
`SalaryRule(UserId, Type {Monthly, Hourly, PercentOfRevenue}, Amount, EffectiveFrom)`. `Payroll(UserId, Period (yil-oy), BaseAmount, Bonuses, Deductions, NetAmount, Status {Draft, Approved, Paid}, PaidAt?)`. **Hisoblash:**
- `Monthly` — `Amount` to'g'ridan-to'g'ri (ta'til/kelmagan kunlar uchun proporsional kamayish — ixtiyoriy birinchi versiyada).
- `Hourly` — `StaffAttendance` dan soatlar × `Amount`.
- `PercentOfRevenue` — o'qituvchining guruhlaridan yig'ilgan `Payment.Amount` (davr ichida, S05.01 workload naqshi) × foiz.
`POST /api/hr/payroll/generate?period=` (`hr.manage` — davr uchun hamma xodim uchun `Payroll` yaratadi, `Draft`), `POST {id}/approve`, `POST {id}/pay` (`salary.manage`).

### 3.5. Ruxsatlar
`hr.view` (o'z ma'lumoti — Self; SA/AD to'liq), `hr.manage` (SA `AllOrganization`, AD `MyBranches`), `salary.view`/`salary.manage` (**faqat SA** — ish haqi eng sezgir ma'lumot, filial administratoriga ham berilmaydi, TZ'da alohida ajratilmagan bo'lsa ham xavfsiz standart).

### 3.6. Frontend
`/hr` bo'limi (faqat `can("hr.view")`): xodimlar ro'yxati (shartnoma, lavozim), davomat kalendari, ta'til so'rovlari (xodimning o'zi uchun ham), `/hr/payroll` (faqat `salary.view`).

## 4. Testlar — `HrTests.cs` (~12, bo'limlarga bo'lib)
