## 1. Maqsad
TZ §25: expenses, cash flow. Hozir faqat kirim (`Payment`) bor, chiqim (ijara, kommunal, marketing, ish haqi, jihoz) yo'q — sof foyda hisoblanmaydi.

## 2. Hozirgi holat
`Modules.Finance/Domain/Payment.cs`, `Api/Finance/FinanceReportsController.cs` (`Summary` — daromad hisoboti, SQL naqshi). S10.07 bo'lsa `Payroll.NetAmount` — xarajat manbai bo'lishi mumkin.

## 3. Nima qilinadi
- **Domen** (migratsiya **`AddExpenses`**): `ExpenseCategory(OrganizationId, Name, IsSystem)` (standart: Ijara, Kommunal, Marketing, Ish haqi, Jihoz, Boshqa — seed qilinadi, `IsSystem=true` o'chirilmaydi), `Expense(OrganizationId, BranchId, CategoryId, Amount, PaidAt, Description, ApprovedBy?, ReceiptFileKey?)`.
- **Endpointlar:** `GET/POST/PATCH/DELETE /api/finance/expenses` (`expenses.manage` — SA `AllOrganization`, AD `MyBranches`; `expenses.view` xuddi shu + kengroq bo'lishi shart emas), kvitansiya fayli (S08.03/S09.06 naqshi, ixtiyoriy).
- **Cash flow hisoboti:** `GET /api/finance/reports/cashflow?from=&to=&branchId=` (`finance.reports.view` — mavjud): oylik jadval — kirim (`Payment`, mavjud `FinanceReportsController` hisobi) − chiqim (`Expense`, + S10.07 `Payroll.NetAmount` agar mavjud bo'lsa) = sof. `FinanceReportsController` ga qo'shiladi (dublikat qilmang).
- **Dashboard:** S05.03 filial dashboardi "Moliya" blokiga sof foyda qatori (ixtiyoriy — asosiy talab emas).
- **Frontend:** `/finance` sahifasiga "Xarajatlar" tabi (ro'yxat, kategoriya bo'yicha filtr, qo'shish formasi), cash flow grafigi (S05.03 `recharts` bo'lsa).

## 4. Testlar — `ExpenseTests.cs` (~7)
1. Xarajat qo'shish/tahrirlash/o'chirish (filial scope). 2. Tizim kategoriyasini o'chirish → 409. 3. Cash flow hisobi: ma'lum kirim+chiqim → aniq sof foyda. 4. Operator/teacher → 403. 5. Boshqa filial xarajati → 403.
